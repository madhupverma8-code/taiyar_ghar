1. About the Platform

TaiyariGhar (taiyarighar.in) is an online examination preparation and mock test portal built with a clear mission: to provide aspirants from villages, towns, and cities across India with an authentic Computer-Based Test (CBT) environment at minimal or zero cost. Every test series on the platform is designed in bilingual format (Hindi and English) according to the latest syllabus, sectional timing, and negative marking rules.

Alongside full-length mock tests, students get access to Free Demo Tests, Previous Year Papers (PYPs), Mini Speed Tests, Daily & Monthly Current Affairs, and Latest Government Job & Syllabus Notifications in one place.

2. Government Exams Covered

TaiyariGhar offers structured test series and practice modules across major central and state recruitment examinations:

  • SSC Examinations: SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD Constable, CPO, and Stenographer.
  • Railway Examinations: RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP, Technician, and RPF Constable / SI.
  • UP Police & Uttar Pradesh State Exams: UP Police Constable, Sub-Inspector (SI), UPSSSC PET, Lekhpal, VDO, RO/ARO, and State PCS.
  • Banking & Insurance Exams: IBPS PO, IBPS Clerk, SBI PO, SBI Clerk, and RRB Office Assistant.
  • Other Central & State Exams: BPSC, UKPSC, JPSC, Defence, Teaching, and State-level recruitment tests.

3. Flagship Feature: All-India Live Exams

All-India Live Tests are scheduled nationwide competitive exams hosted on TaiyariGhar where candidates across India appear simultaneously under a strict anti-cheating exam interface (mandatory full-screen mode and tab-switch protection). Once the scheduled live exam window closes, All-India Ranks, percentiles, accuracy reports, and complete question-by-question solutions are released simultaneously on every participant's dashboard.

4. Unlimited Smart Practice Bank

To help students build daily speed and accuracy without taking a full mock test every time, TaiyariGhar provides the Unlimited Smart Practice Bank featuring thousands of Previous Year Questions (PYQs) and Expected Questions across Hindi, English, Mathematics, Reasoning, General Science (GS), and General Knowledge (GK).

  • Topic-Wise & Mix Practice Modes: Candidates can practice specific chapters (such as Vilom Shabd, Paryayvachi, Muhavre, Percentage, Coding-Decoding) or launch Mix Practice that combines questions from all topics automatically.
  • Instant Solutions & Speed Tracking: Selecting an option immediately reveals the correct answer, detailed explanation, and per-question time taken, while automatically separating Attempted and Not Attempted questions so solved questions never repeat unnecessarily.
  • Free & Pro Access: Registered students can practice the first 15 questions of every topic completely free, while unlimited access to the entire question bank unlocks with an active All-Access Pass.

5. Subscription Plans & Secure Payment Process

Students can evaluate the platform using free mock tests and free practice sets before upgrading. Complete access to all mock tests, live test solutions, and the Unlimited Smart Practice Bank is available under three affordable All-Access Subscription Passes:

  • 3 Months Pass (90 Days): Complete access to all exam categories, live exams, and practice modules for 90 days.
  • 6 Months Pass (180 Days): Extended preparation access across all central and state test series for 180 days.
  • 12 Months Pass (365 Days): Full-year unlimited access to all existing and upcoming test series and question banks.

How Payment Works: All subscription payments are processed securely via Razorpay Payment Gateway supporting UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Debit/Credit Cards, and Net Banking. As soon as a payment is completed, the subscription activates automatically on the student's account and an official payment receipt/invoice is emailed to their registered email address. TaiyariGhar never stores card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs.

6. Official Referral Partner Program

Through the TaiyariGhar Official Referral Partner Program, teachers, content creators, study group admins, and students can register as verified partners and receive a unique referral coupon code. When a student applies a partner's coupon code during pass checkout, the student receives an instant discount plus bonus validity days, while the partner earns a transparent commission on every successful enrollment, settled directly via bank transfer or UPI on the last date of every month.
