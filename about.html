1. About the Platform

TaiyariGhar (taiyarighar.in) is an online examination preparation and mock test portal built to give aspirants from villages, towns, and cities across India an authentic Computer-Based Test (CBT) environment. Tests and practice modules are provided in bilingual format (Hindi and English) for mobile and laptop users.

Alongside mock tests, the platform includes All-India Live Tests, Unlimited Smart Practice Bank, NCERT Base Question Bank (Class 6–12), Free Subject-Wise Previous Year Questions (PYQs), Current Affairs, Study Notes, and Latest Govt Jobs, Admit Cards, Results & Syllabus.

2. Government Exams Covered

  • SSC Exams: SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD Constable, CPO, and Stenographer.
  • Railway Exams: RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP, Technician, and RPF Constable / SI.
  • UP Police & State Exams: UP Police Constable, Sub-Inspector (SI), UPSSSC PET, Lekhpal, VDO, RO/ARO, and State PCS.
  • Banking & Insurance: IBPS PO, IBPS Clerk, SBI PO, SBI Clerk, and RRB Office Assistant.
  • Other Central & State Exams: BPSC, UKPSC, JPSC, Defence, Teaching, and State recruitment tests.

3. CBT Timers, Time-Management Heatmap & Mistake Notebook

  • Per-Question Timer & Sectional Timer: Tracks exact seconds spent per question during CBT exams (highlighting slow questions over 2 minutes) alongside section-wise timers and tab transitions where multiple sections exist.
  • Time-Management Heatmap: Result scorecards show per-question speed color blocks, average time per question, and count of slow (>120s) questions.
  • Mistake Notebook & Quiz Challenge: Wrong and slow (>2 min) questions are auto-saved for revision, and students can share quiz challenge links or personal referral links with friends.

4. Practice Bank, NCERT Base & Free Subject-Wise PYQs

  • Unlimited Smart Practice Bank: Topic-wise and Mix practice across subjects with instant bilingual explanations and speed stats (first 15 questions per subject free for registered students; practice bank tags use Expected Exam labels).
  • NCERT Base Question Bank (Class 6–12): Chapter-wise NCERT-aligned questions in Hindi and English.
  • Subject-Wise PYQs (No Login Required): Free public viewer with subject tabs, exam/year tags, highlighted answers, and explanations.

5. All-India Live Exams, Jobs/Updates & Navigation

  • All-India Live Exams: Scheduled nationwide tests with anti-cheating controls and rank/solution release on the dashboard after exam closing time.
  • Jobs, Admit Cards, Results, Syllabus, Current Affairs & Study Notes: Dedicated sections accessible from the top navigation bar (supporting deep links and new tabs) with step-to-step browser back navigation and Light/Dark mode.

6. Subscription Passes, Referrals & Partner Program

  • All-Access Subscription Passes: Available in 3 Months (90 days), 6 Months (180 days), and 12 Months (365 days) options via Razorpay (UPI, Cards, Net Banking) with instant activation and email invoice.
  • Student Refer & Earn & Partner Program: Students can refer friends for coupon discounts/bonus days and earn +20 bonus pass days on qualifying referrals; educators and creators can join via the Referral Partner Portal.
