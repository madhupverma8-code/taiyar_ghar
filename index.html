Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What are the subscription plans and discounts?

We offer flexible 3-Month, 6-Month, and 12-Month All-Access passes with special festival/launch discounts. Click on "Get All-Access Subscription" or any locked test to view active offers.

How do Live Tests work?

Live Tests are scheduled for a specific time window for subscribed members. Just like real exams, your result and All-India Rank are published after the live window ends (12:00 AM) and sent directly to your registered Email ID.

How can I make the payment?

We accept instant online payments via Razorpay (UPI, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, Cards, and Net Banking). Your account unlocks automatically upon payment and an official PDF invoice is emailed to you.

What if I forget my password?

Don't worry! You can easily reset your password using a 6-digit OTP sent to your registered Email ID.

Can I use my account on multiple devices?

Your account can be used on up to 2 devices simultaneously. If you want to switch to a new device, you can log in, and it will automatically replace the oldest device.
