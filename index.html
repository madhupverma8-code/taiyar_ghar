Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 | Applicable to www.taiyarighar.in

At TaiyariGhar, protecting your personal privacy is our top priority. This policy outlines how we collect, protect, and use your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Number, and test performance records (scores, accuracy, and ranks) solely to provide mock test services and personalized analytics.

2. Razorpay Payment Security

All payments are processed securely via Razorpay. TaiyariGhar never stores your card details, CVV, or UPI PIN. We only store your Razorpay Payment ID and Invoice Number to activate your pass and email your PDF receipt.

3. Email OTP & Communication

Your email is used for 6-digit OTP verification, account credentials, PDF invoice delivery, and All-India Live Test scorecards. We never sell or rent student data to third parties.

Effective Date: November 2026 | Platform: www.taiyarighar.in

Each subscription pass is strictly for individual use and may be accessed on a maximum of 2 devices. Sharing login credentials publicly may lead to account suspension.

2. Live Exam Fair-Play Rules

Tab switching, exiting full-screen mode, or taking screenshots during All-India Live Exams is strictly prohibited. Three warnings will trigger automatic test submission.

3. Intellectual Property

All test questions, explanations, and study materials on TaiyariGhar are protected by copyright. Unauthorized copying or redistribution is prohibited.

Refund & Cancellation Policy
