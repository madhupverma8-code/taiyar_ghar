Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the price of the subscription?

Our subscription is very affordable. You can check the exact price by clicking on any locked test or in the "My Purchases" section.

What are the subscription plans available?

We offer flexible Monthly and Quarterly (3 months) subscription plans. You can choose the plan that best suits your preparation timeline.

How can I make the payment?

We accept payments via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). You just need to scan the QR code shown on the payment screen and send a screenshot on WhatsApp for instant activation.

What if I forget my password?

Don't worry! We provide a secure Recovery Code and Security Question when you register. You can use either of them to reset your password.

Can I use my account on multiple devices?

Your account can be used on up to 2 devices simultaneously. If you want to switch to a new device, you can log in, and it will automatically replace the oldest device.
