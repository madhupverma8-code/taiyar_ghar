Careers — Official Referral Partner Program

Partner with TaiyariGhar, help exam aspirants save on test passes, and earn transparent monthly commissions.

1. What is the Official Referral Partner Program?

The TaiyariGhar Official Referral Partner Program is a zero-fee affiliate initiative for students, educators, YouTubers, Telegram/WhatsApp community admins, and local coaching operators. You can share your unique referral coupon code with government exam aspirants and earn a fixed commission on every successful subscription purchase.

2. How It Works & How You Earn

  • Unique Partner ID & Coupon Code: Once verified, the system assigns you a unique Partner ID (e.g., TGP-1001) and a custom Referral Coupon Code (e.g., RAHUL78).
  • Double Benefit for Students: When a student applies your coupon code during checkout, they receive an instant fee discount along with bonus validity days on their subscription plan.
  • Commission on Every Successful Payment: You earn 10% commission (or the rate configured by Admin) on the final paid amount whenever a student completes a Razorpay payment using your code.
  • Renewal Commissions Included: When an existing student renews their expired plan using your coupon code, you earn commission on the renewal payment as well.
  • Read-Only Partner Dashboard: Log in anytime to track your coupon usage count, masked student purchase history, total commission generated, and payout status.

3. Monthly Payout Schedule

Direct Bank / UPI Payout: All verified commission earnings are transferred directly to your registered Bank Account (NEFT/IMPS) or UPI ID on the last date of every month.

4. Eligibility & Required Details

  • Own Bank Account Mandatory: Only candidates who hold their own active personal bank account may register. Using a friend's, relative's, or third-party bank account will result in immediate rejection.
  • No Hard-Copy Document Upload Needed: You do not need to upload Aadhaar or PAN documents. You only need: Full Name (matching your bank passbook), Active Mobile Number, Gmail ID, City/State, Bank Account Holder Name, Bank Account Number, IFSC Code, and UPI ID.
  • Promotion Link (Optional): You may optionally provide your YouTube, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook, or coaching details.

5. Registration & 24-Hour Verification Process

  • Step 1 (OTP Verification): Verify your Mobile Number and Gmail ID using a 6-digit OTP.
  • Step 2 (Submit Application): Fill out your personal and bank payout details, accept all Terms & Conditions, and submit the form.
  • Step 3 (Admin Verification within 24 Hours): Your profile enters Verification Pending status. Team TaiyariGhar verifies that your applicant name matches your Bank Account Holder Name within 24 hours.
  • Step 4 (Activation): Once approved, your Partner Dashboard and Unique Coupon Code become active immediately.

6. Terms & Conditions

  • Strictly one Partner ID per individual (enforced via Mobile, Gmail, Bank Account Number, and UPI ID).
  • Coupon usage is counted only after 100% successful Razorpay payment confirmation.
  • Misleading claims, spam promotion, or fake details will result in permanent suspension of your Partner ID.
